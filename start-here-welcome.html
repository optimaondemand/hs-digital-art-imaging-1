Welcome to Digital Art Imaging
Course Orientation
👋 WELCOME

This year you will learn to make images on purpose. You will take and edit photographs, build digital collages, design with type, draw and animate on screen, and finish with a portfolio of work that is genuinely yours. The tools are modern, but the goal is old: say something with an image, and say it well.

Everything runs in your web browser with free software, so you can work from anywhere, at your own pace. Before the first lesson, spend this short orientation getting set up. Four quick activities, and then you are ready to create.

✅ YOUR FIRST WEEK, STEP BY STEP
  • 1
    Course Scavenger Hunt · Quiz
    Find your way around the course so nothing surprises you later.
  • 2
    Plan Your Time · Assignment
    Block out when you will do this course's work in the Study Planner.
  • 3
    Know Your Support · Read
    Learn how to reach your facilitator and get help before you need it.
  • 4
    Introduce Yourself · Discussion
    Meet the class by sharing an image that represents you.
🛠️ WHAT YOU WILL NEED
A device with a web browser, and a way to take photos (a phone or any digital camera). Your editing software is Pixlr E, free at pixlr.com/e, with nothing to install. You will also keep a portfolio folder in your school OneDrive named DigitalArt_Portfolio_LastName. Set that up in the Scavenger Hunt and your first lesson.
🎨 HOW THE YEAR IS BUILT
Four quarters, each building on the last: Foundations & Photography, then Capturing Light & Editing, then Collage & Typography, then Drawing, Animation & Portfolio. Each week has one short lesson to learn from and one project to make. Save every finished piece; you will curate your strongest work into a final portfolio in the last weeks.
💪 ONE THING TO REMEMBER

You do not need to be a "natural artist" to do well here. Every skill in this course is learnable, and every project rewards thoughtful choices over natural talent. Start the five activities below, and welcome to the studio.
